## What is VideoShareProBot?

**VideoShareProBot** is a free Telegram bot for downloading and sharing 18+ videos. No sign-up, no OTP, no subscription, no daily limit. Send a link — get the video back in seconds.

The bot runs on a library of **300,000+ clips** covering Desi, viral, leaked and exclusive categories, plus a dedicated Bengali collection. Everything is free and always will be.

> ⚠️ 18+ only. All videos are hosted by third parties and remain the property of their original owners.

## How to use

1. Open [@VideoShareProBot](https://t.me/VideoShareProBot) on Telegram
2. Press **Start**
3. Set your interests (one time, optional)
4. Send a video link, or browse the library
5. The bot replies with the video — download or forward it

To save something, tap **⭐ Favourite** on any result.

### Adding to a group

1. Open your group → **Add members**
2. Search `@VideoShareProBot`
3. Grant it permission to send messages and media
4. Done — members can now use it in the group

## FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. No cost, no trial, no premium tier.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Open the bot and press Start. That's the whole setup.

**How many videos are there?**
Over 300,000 and growing daily.

**Is there a download limit?**
No daily or hourly cap.

**Can I use it in a group?**
Yes. See [Adding to a group](#adding-to-a-group).

**What if a link fails?**
Send `/report` followed by the link, or open an issue here.

**Why do some videos not play in Telegram?**
Telegram compresses large files. Use the download button to get the original.

**Does it store my data?**
The bot keeps only your Telegram user ID and your interest/favourite list. No email, no phone number.
